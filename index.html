Warm Up

Essential Question: How does the timeliness and quality of professional communication influence trust, relationships, and business outcomes?

By the end of this lesson, you will learn:

  • How silence after a message shapes what customers, employees, applicants, and partners believe about an organization
  • How to write a professional acknowledgment that confirms receipt, shows respect, and sets a realistic next step
  • How to triage a full inbox so you acknowledge what matters without living in your email
  • How leaders set team communication expectations that build accountability and protect personal time

      Round 3

      The Inbox Reality Check

      You just walked out of a morning of meetings. It is 11:52 AM and there are 25 unread emails. Should you answer everything right now? Absolutely not. Sort each one into an action.

      Your four actions

      Reply fully now: urgent and quick to answer.
      Acknowledge now, reply later: needs research or time, but the sender should know you have it.
      Automated acknowledgment: high volume or routine; a standard auto-reply serves the sender well.
      No reply needed: announcements, FYIs, and marketing.
      Twenty-five unread emails to triage
